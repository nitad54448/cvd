2. Measured Optimums & Weighting

Both responses are maximized. Weights are normalized to sum to 100%; all-zero weights disable the combined score. Scores use each response’s measured minimum and maximum, so adding data can change earlier scores. A constant response contributes 0.5. A score requires all positively weighted responses. Predicted scores may fall outside 0–1.

4. Predicted Optimum Conditions
